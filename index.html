The Dram Journal – your personal whisky tasting journal

Every dram deserves a story. The Dram Journal is a free whisky logbook: track tastings with nose, palate and star ratings, build a wishlist, compare bottles side by side, and share drams with fellow enthusiasts.

What's inside

  • Tasting journal – log every bottle with notes, photos and ratings.
  • Wishlist – track bottles you want to try and share them with a link.
  • Bottle scanner – point your camera at a label to auto-fill details.
  • Compare – put up to three whiskies side by side.
  • Stats & badges – see the countries, distilleries and styles you've explored.
  • Friends & sharing – follow other collectors and send recommendations.
